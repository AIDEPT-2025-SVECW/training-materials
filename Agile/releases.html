Topic 04

Releases & Version Milestones

Strategic release management, MVP scoping, and customer value milestones.

What is a Release in Agile?

An Agile Release is a major version deployment or milestone container that bundles several completed Epics and User Stories into a production-ready package for end users.

💡 What is an MVP (Minimum Viable Product)?

An MVP is the first release of a product containing only the absolute minimum features required to satisfy early customers and validate business hypotheses with real feedback.

TastyDash Food Ordering Release Roadmap

🚀 Release 1.0 — MVP Launch

Target: Sprint 1 - 3 (Month 1)

Release Objective: Validate basic food ordering demand in pilot area with minimum engineering complexity.

Release Scope & Included Epics:
  • ✅ Customer Registration via Email & Phone OTP
  • ✅ Search & Browse 5 partner restaurants in pilot zip code
  • ✅ Simple shopping cart subtotal calculation
  • ✅ Payment Mode: Cash on Delivery (COD) only
  • ✅ SMS order status notification (Order Placed, Out for Delivery)

⚡ Release 2.0 — Digital Payments & GPS Tracking

Target: Sprint 4 - 7 (Month 3)

Release Objective: Scale to city-wide operations with seamless digital payments and driver tracking.

Release Scope & Included Epics:
  • ✅ Stripe Payment Integration (Credit Card, Debit Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
  • ✅ Real-Time Driver GPS Tracking on Google Maps
  • ✅ Customer ratings and restaurant reviews
  • ✅ Multi-address management (Saved Home, Work, Gym addresses)

🤖 Release 3.0 — AI Personalization & Loyalty

Target: Sprint 8 - 12 (Month 6)

Release Objective: Maximize customer retention, average order value, and viral growth.

Release Scope & Included Epics:
  • ✅ AI-powered "Food You Might Like" personal recommendation carousel
  • ✅ Dynamic discount promo codes & coupon engine
  • ✅ Refer-a-friend loyalty rewards points
  • ✅ Group Order mode (share cart link with office colleagues)
