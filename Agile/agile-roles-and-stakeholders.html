Topic 08

Agile Roles & Stakeholders

Understanding the core Scrum roles, cross-functional engineering teams, and business stakeholders in real software delivery.

The 3 Pillars: Core Scrum Team Roles

In Scrum, accountability is divided among three distinct roles without traditional hierarchical micro-management. Each role owns a specific dimension of the product.

🎯

Product Owner (PO)

Owns the "What" & "Why"

Maximizes the value of the product. Manages the Product Backlog, writes User Stories, defines Acceptance Criteria, and prioritizes feature delivery.

🍔 TastyDash PO: Decides that adding Stripe Credit Card payment is more urgent than Drone delivery for Sprint 3.
🛡️

Scrum Master (SM)

Owns the "Process & Flow"

A servant leader who coaches the team on Scrum practices, removes impediments (blockers), and ensures ceremonies are productive.

🍔 TastyDash SM: Removes a blocker when backend developers are delayed waiting for payment API credentials.
💻

Developers / Engineering Team

Owns the "How" & "Quality"

Cross-functional team of Software Engineers (Frontend, Backend), QA Testers, UI/UX Designers, and DevOps who build working increment software.

🍔 TastyDash Devs: React engineers, Java Spring Boot developers, and QA automation testers building checkout features.

Extended Agile Stakeholders

Beyond the core Scrum team, software delivery relies on active engagement with internal business units and external users.

💼 Executive Sponsors & Leadership

Provide budget, overall business direction, and strategic milestones for TastyDash.

😋 End Users & Customers

Hungry food ordering users who test releases and provide feedback after every sprint demo.

🏬 Restaurant & Fleet Partners

Restaurant owners updating menus and delivery partners fulfilling food delivery orders.

🔐 Security & Compliance SMEs

Auditors ensuring PCI-DSS payment security compliance and GST tax calculation rules.

RACI Matrix: Roles & Responsibilities

R = Responsible (Does the work), A = Accountable (Final decision maker), C = Consulted (Provides input), I = Informed (Kept updated).
